About EduBoost Nepal

We're on a mission to make world-class exam preparation accessible to every NEB student across Nepal — from Kathmandu to Jumla.

Our Story

EduBoost Nepal was founded in 2024 by a group of young Nepali educators and technologists who saw a clear gap: despite the high-stakes nature of NEB board exams, most students relied on outdated notes, expensive private tutoring, or guesswork about exam patterns.

We built EduBoost Nepal to change that. By combining modern AI technology with deep knowledge of the NEB curriculum, we created a platform where any Class 11 or Class 12 student — regardless of location or economic background — can access personalised study tools, high-quality notes, and intelligent exam-preparation guidance.

Today, EduBoost Nepal serves students across Nepal with free AI-powered study tools, chapter-wise notes, downloadable PDFs, and detailed blog guides covering every major NEB subject from Physics and Chemistry to Mathematics, Computer Science, and English.

What makes EduBoost Nepal different is its laser focus on the NEB curriculum specifically. Every question generated by the AI, every blog article, and every set of notes is built around the actual NEB syllabus and recent exam patterns — not generic content repurposed for a Nepali audience. The AI tutor understands NEB marking schemes and can explain concepts the way an NEB examiner would expect to see them answered.

Behind EduBoost Nepal

EduBoost Nepal is an independent project built by Nabin, a Class 12 Science graduate from Kathmandu who completed his NEB board exams at Paramount Academy. Having experienced firsthand how difficult it is to find well-organised, curriculum-aligned study resources in Nepal, he decided to build the platform he wished he had during his own exam preparation.

The entire platform — from the AI tutor backend and Firebase authentication to the blog system and PDF notes — is designed, built, and maintained as a solo project. Every blog article, every study guide, and every feature is created with one goal in mind: to give NEB students a genuinely useful tool that is actually aligned with the current NEB syllabus and exam patterns.

EduBoost Nepal uses Google Gemini AI to power the question generator and AI tutor, with a focus on producing board-exam-style answers that reflect real NEB marking criteria. The platform is free to use and will remain free, funded only by non-intrusive advertising.
