1 The NEB Book Hierarchy (Read This First)

Not all books are equal for NEB exams. Here's the exact priority order every topper follows:

Priority Book Type Why When to Use
🔴 #1 HSEB/NEB Prescribed Textbook Exam questions come directly from here Always — start here
🟠 #2 Past Question Papers (10 years) Pattern recognition, exact question types After reading each chapter
🔵 #3 Subject-Specific Guide Books Simplified theory + solved exercises When textbook is unclear
🟢 #4 Online Resources / YouTube Visual concepts, quick revision For tough topics only
⚪ #5 Indian Reference Books (HC Verma etc.) Deep knowledge, NOT exam-optimized for NEB Only if you aim for IOE/MOE entrance
⚠️ Critical Mistake Students Make

Buying expensive Indian reference books and spending 80% of their time on them — while NEB examiner literally copies questions from the Nepali textbook exercises. Don't be that student.

5 What Toppers Do vs. What Average Students Do

✅ Do This (Topper Habits)
  • Solve past 10 years questions for every chapter
  • Write answers — don't just read them
  • Focus on definitions and key terms — NEB loves exact wording
  • Make a formula/reaction sheet for last-minute revision
  • Practice diagrams until you can draw them from memory
  • Do 1 full mock exam per week in the last month
  • Spend 70% time on high-mark chapters (check NEB weightage)
❌ Avoid This (Common Mistakes)
  • Reading HC Verma fully for NEB board exam
  • Buying 4+ books per subject — stick to 2 max
  • Highlighting without writing/practicing
  • Skipping back exercises thinking they won't come
  • Starting new topics 1 week before exams
  • Memorizing without understanding reactions
  • Ignoring English and Nepali (free marks!)

6 Smart Study Strategy — Month by Month

Shrawan–Ashwin (Class Start)
Read each chapter from the NEB textbook. Understand concepts. Don't memorize yet. Watch YouTube for unclear topics.
Kartik–Poush (Mid-Year)
Solve all exercises in the guide book. Start collecting past question papers. Identify frequently asked questions per chapter.
Magh–Falgun (3 Months Before)
Past paper practice every week. Make formula sheets. Practice 5-mark long answers. Target weakest chapters now.
Chaitra (Last Month)
Full mock exams under time pressure. Quick revision using your formula/definition sheets only. No new topics.
Exam Week
Only use your summary notes. Sleep 7+ hours. Eat well. Don't open new chapters. Trust your preparation.

7 NEB Exam Strategy — Marking Tips

⏱️
Time Management
Spend ~1.2 min per mark. 3-hour exam = 100 marks total. Don't spend 20 min on a 2-mark question.
✍️
Answer Format
Always start with definition, then explanation, then diagram (if applicable), then conclusion. This structure = full marks.
📊
Diagram Power
A neat, labeled diagram in Biology or Physics can earn 2–3 marks on its own. Always draw diagrams for 5-mark questions.
🔢
Show All Steps
In Math and Physics, partial marks are given for steps even if final answer is wrong. Never skip working.
📝
Attempt Everything
NEB has no negative marking. Attempt every question. A blank answer = 0. Even partial writing may earn 1 mark.
🎯
High-Mark First
Attempt questions you know well first. This builds confidence and ensures you don't run out of time on easy marks.
💡 Examiner Insight
  • NEB examiners are generous with partial marks — always write something
  • Neat handwriting genuinely helps — messy answers get less scrutiny from examiners
  • Exact textbook language in definitions = guaranteed full marks
  • For 2-mark questions: 2 clear points, no rambling needed
  • For 5-mark questions: intro + 3–4 points + diagram + conclusion
