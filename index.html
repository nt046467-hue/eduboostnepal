About EduBoost Nepal

Nepal's First AI-Powered NEB Exam Prep Platform

Why EduBoost Nepal Exists

Preparing for the NEB board exam has always been difficult for students outside of Kathmandu's top private schools. Expensive tuition centres, photocopied notes with unclear sources, and a lack of structured guidance on what the examiners actually want — these are problems that affect hundreds of thousands of students across Nepal every year.

EduBoost Nepal was built to address this gap. Using Google Gemini AI, the platform generates NEB-style questions, structured board-exam answers, and chapter-wise explanations aligned specifically with the National Examinations Board syllabus for Class 11 and Class 12. Every feature is designed around one question: what does an NEB student actually need to score higher on their board exam?

📐 How the AI Question Generator Works

Select your subject, chapter, and question type (MCQ, short answer, or long answer). The AI generates questions at the correct difficulty level and format for NEB board exams — including common numerical problems for Physics and Math that mirror past paper patterns.

📝 Board-Style Answer Writing

One of the most common reasons students lose marks is poor answer structure. The AI tutor on EduBoost Nepal is specifically prompted to write answers the way NEB examiners expect — with proper headings, point-format explanations, and diagrams where relevant.

📚 NEB Subjects Covered

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Nepali, Economics, Accountancy, and Social Studies — all for NEB Class 11 and Class 12. Content is organised by class, subject, and chapter for easy navigation.

🇳🇵 Built for Nepal

EduBoost Nepal is not a generic study app with a Nepali flag added. Every piece of content — from blog articles to AI tutor prompts — is specifically calibrated to the NEB curriculum, recent exam patterns, and the Nepali education context.

Study Smarter, Not Harder — What That Actually Means for NEB Students

The NEB board exam tests your ability to recall, apply, and explain concepts clearly. Most students spend their study time passively re-reading notes, which is one of the least effective ways to prepare. Active recall — testing yourself with questions, writing practice answers, and identifying gaps — is far more effective for long-term retention.

EduBoost Nepal's tools are designed around active recall. Use the AI Question Generator to test yourself, the AI Tutor to check your understanding, and the downloadable notes as a reference when you need to fill gaps — not as your primary source of study. Combine this with our blog guides on exam strategy, and you have a complete, structured approach to NEB preparation that actually works.
